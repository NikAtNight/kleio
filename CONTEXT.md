# Kleio

Kleio keeps recordings and their transcripts on the Mac, with separate microphone and remote audio.

## Language

**Recording**: Saved meeting or memo media, its transcript, speaker assignments, notes, and optional summary.

**Remote speaker**: A person heard in app audio. The microphone remains a separate, fixed identity.

**Pending save**: Captured media or a completed transcript or summary whose latest result has not yet been saved successfully.

**Speaker correction**: A user's rename, assignment, or merge of detected speaker labels. It preserves the recorded audio and transcript text.

**Library backup**: A restorable copy of recording media, transcripts, notes, people, and local preferences. Downloaded models and credentials are separate from the backup.
