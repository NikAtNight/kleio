Kleio records you and the call, then writes it all down. Stay in the conversation. The words will be waiting when you need them.

See how it works ↓

Two tracks.
One conversation.

Your mic and the call each get their own track, so your words are always labeled as yours. Search the transcript, fix a speaker's name, or jump back to the moment it was said. Add notes, then export.

Example conversation

You 02:14

"Let's keep the import fix in this release."

Priya 02:21

"I'll take the watch folder retry."

Your call.
Your copy.

Recording, transcription, and speaker labels all happen on your own computer. No bot joins the meeting, and there's no account to make. Cloud summaries are optional and only send transcript text to the provider you pick.
